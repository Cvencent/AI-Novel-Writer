# 1.5.19

## 中文

- 修复 `ARC_OUTLINE_REVISION_STALE`：确认粗纲段、按段生成细纲、按方向重写粗纲段之前都会先重新读取最新修订号。
- 确认时若仍遇到修订号过期，会自动刷新并重试一次，不再直接报错。
- 面板列表与主进程数据保持一致，避免用旧列表里的 revision 去提交。

## English

- Fixed `ARC_OUTLINE_REVISION_STALE`: confirming an arc segment, generating chapter outlines for a segment, and rewriting a segment now re-read the latest revision before submitting.
- A stale confirm automatically refreshes and retries once instead of failing outright.
- The panel now stays in sync with the authoritative revision instead of submitting a stale one.

The Windows installer is not code-signed.
