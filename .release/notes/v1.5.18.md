# 1.5.18

## 中文

- 粗纲支持多选删除：勾选任意段后点删除，会从最早选中的那段起删除该段及之后的所有粗纲段，随后可从缺口处重新生成。
- 细纲右侧目录支持多选删除：勾选章节后一键删除所选章节块，正文与目录同步更新，可重新生成。
- 两处都支持全选，删除前有确认提示。

## English

- Arc segments can be multi-selected and deleted; deleting removes the earliest selected segment and everything after it, so regeneration can resume from the gap.
- Chapter outline entries can be multi-selected and deleted from the right-hand contents; the document and the contents update together and can be regenerated.
- Both support select-all and ask for confirmation before deleting.

The Windows installer is not code-signed.
