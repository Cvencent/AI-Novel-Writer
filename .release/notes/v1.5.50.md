# 1.5.50

## 中文

### 修复：粗纲铺不满全书，卡在「ARC_SPAN_TOO_SMALL」

- 粗纲每段过去被硬性要求至少 20 章。可是「一键生成全部粗纲」要一段接一段铺到全书末章，最后一段往往只剩十几章（例如 40 章的书切到第 23 章起只剩 18 章）；12 章、15 章这类短书更是第一段就不足 20 章。这时模型按提示词收口到末章也会被校验判死，粗纲永远铺不满，界面只留一个失败。
- 现在收口段可以短于 20 章，只要它铺到全书末章就放行；中段若想用「快收官了」当借口把段落切碎，仍然会被拒。提示词里「剩余章节不足上限时，可以生成到全书末章」这句话，终于和校验口径一致了。
- 顺带修掉了这个错误的一个连锁反应：反推粗纲（从已有细纲倒推）也会因为同样的段长校验整体失败，现在同样按收口规则放行末段。

### 修复：恢复副本里「同代旧回包」可能覆盖更新的章

- 从备份/云恢复出来的副本，角色的 derived 字段带着原项目的连续性 epoch。过去只要新旧 epoch 字符串不同、且 generation 计数器「不小于」来源，就整段跳过章/修订排序，于是同一代的旧回包（例如原项目 `:2` 的旧数据先到、目标项目 `:2` 的新数据后到）会反过来盖掉更新的章。
- 现在区分「generation 严格推进」与「generation 持平」：只有严格推进才不再按章排序；持平的只说明记法对得上，仍然走章/修订比较，旧回包会被判冲突。

## English

### Fixed: arc outline could not cover the whole book, stuck on "ARC_SPAN_TOO_SMALL"

- Every arc segment was hard-required to span at least 20 chapters. But "Generate all arc segments" runs segment after segment until the book is covered, and the final segment usually has only a dozen-plus chapters left (a 40-chapter book starting its last segment at chapter 23 has just 18). Short books of 12 or 15 chapters fail on the very first segment. The model followed the prompt ("if fewer chapters remain, you may run to the final chapter") and was then rejected by the validator, so the arc outline could never cover the whole book and the UI showed only a failure.
- The closing segment may now be shorter than 20 chapters as long as it reaches the final chapter; a mid-book segment still cannot be chopped short by claiming it is about to close the book. The prompt line "if fewer chapters remain, you may run to the final chapter" now matches what the validator actually accepts.
- Also fixed a knock-on effect: reverse-generated arcs (inferred from an existing fine outline) failed the same span check and now accept a closing short segment under the same rule.

### Fixed: a same-generation stale packet could overwrite a newer chapter in restored copies

- Character fields derived in a backup/cloud-restored copy carry the origin project's continuity epoch. The previous check skipped chapter/revision ordering whenever the two epoch strings differed and the generation counter was merely "not less than" the origin's, so a same-generation stale packet (origin `:2` arriving before, target `:2` arriving after) could overwrite a newer chapter.
- The check now distinguishes "strictly newer generation" from "equal generation": only a strictly newer generation drops the chapter ordering; an equal generation only proves the notation lines up and still gets the chapter/revision comparison, so the stale packet conflicts.
